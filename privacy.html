Privacy Policy

Transparency is our priority. Learn how we handle your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal data to provide our services. This includes:

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to:

3. Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase for authentication and database management. Firebase may collect certain device identifiers to ensure security and app stability. Your data is encrypted and is never sold to third-party advertisers.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your information as long as your account is active. Under the DPDP Act 2023, you have the right to withdraw consent. You can request account deletion at any time by emailing us at kamayegabharat@gmail.com.
