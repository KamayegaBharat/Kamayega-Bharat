How Kamayega Bharat Works

Simple, fast, and completely free. Connecting India's workers and employers — no middlemen, no hidden fees.

About the Platform

Built for every worker in India

Most job portals are built for corporate India. Kamayega Bharat is built for everyone else — the millions of hardworking people across every state and city who deserve a simple and reliable way to find work and get hired. Whether you are looking for your first job or hiring your next team member, Kamayega Bharat gets it done in minutes — for free. The platform is openly developed and hosted on GitHub.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Powerful tools for both job seekers and employers — all in one place.

🔍 For Job Seekers

  • Search and filter jobs by city, job type, and work mode
  • See which jobs match your skills best
  • Apply to any job with a single click
  • Track your application — Applied, Review, Interview, Hired
  • Chat directly with employers in real time
  • Join interview calls through Google Meet
  • Download your official hiring letter as a PDF
  • Upload your resume and certificates via Google Drive link
  • Get notified instantly when your application status changes

🏢 For Employers

  • Post a job listing in under 2 minutes — for free
  • Receive and manage all applications in one place
  • View each applicant's skills, experience, and resume
  • Chat directly with candidates
  • Schedule interviews by sharing a Google Meet link
  • System issues a verifiable selection letter automatically on hire
  • Verify any selection letter using its reference number
  • Get a Verified Employer badge to build trust with job seekers

Get Started

Getting Started as a Job Seeker

From signup to your first application in under 2 minutes.

1

Visit the portal

Go to kamayegabharat.github.io on your phone or computer.

2

Sign up with your email

Create your free account in seconds. No verification delays.

3

Fill in your profile

Add your skills, city, and a short intro about yourself. Takes about 2 minutes.

4

Browse jobs and apply

Filter by your city and job type. Apply to matching jobs with one click.

5

Chat and get hired

Talk directly to the employer, attend your interview, and track your status live.

Getting Started as an Employer
