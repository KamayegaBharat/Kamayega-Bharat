The Mission Behind the Platform

The story of Kamayega Bharat begins with a simple belief: every worker in India deserves a direct path to employment — no middlemen, no hidden fees. This open initiative is built to make that possible for millions of job seekers across every state and city.

The Developer Behind the Code

The code behind Kamayega Bharat is written by Sourav Bagchi with a focus on clean logic, security, and high performance for India's workforce. No fluff — just direct connections between real people and real opportunities.
